# EV Owner Landscape — Sri Lanka (research, 2026-06-25)

Survey of apps, websites, and resources already available to EV owners in Sri Lanka, to find
where DriveVault's **EV ownership companion** (strategy direction 3) fits and what already exists.

## What already exists

### Charging networks & maps (well covered)
- **chargeNET / chargeNET Plus** (by Vega Innovations) — the dominant network, **600+ chargers**
  island-wide. The **chargeNET Plus** app (iOS & Android) finds stations, shows real-time
  availability, starts charging remotely, handles payments/top-up, charging history, favourites.
  This is the category leader for *charging*. → chargenet.lk
- **Place to Plug** — crowd/aggregator charging-station map covering Sri Lanka. → placetoplug.com
- **evclub.lk map** — PHEV/EV charging stations, centres, and maintenance services map.
- **CEB / LECO** — utility-operated public chargers (LECO fast charging) alongside private operators.

### Community
- **EV Club Sri Lanka (evclub.lk)** — formed 2015 to promote EVs and support owners; knowledge
  sharing on maintenance and ownership. Active **Facebook groups** (e.g. "Electric Vehicles Club
  Sri Lanka" / EVusersSriLanka) where owners discuss charging rates, conversions, infrastructure.

### Cost / tooling
- **induwara.lk EV charging cost calculator** — takes car + daily distance + charging method,
  returns monthly LKR on CEB **D-1** block tariff or **D-TOU** time-of-use, compared to petrol/
  diesel pump price; computes true **marginal** EV cost via the PUCSL slab schedule.
- **Hybrid & EV buying guides** (lankawebsites.com) — taxes, charging, running-cost guides aimed
  at buyers in the post-import-ban / EV-tax-relief 2026 market.

## The gap (where DriveVault fits)
The SL EV ecosystem is strong on **charging (find/pay)** and **community (Facebook)**, and has
**one-off cost calculators** — but nobody owns the **personal EV ownership logbook**:
- No app keeps *your* car's running history: kWh logged per charge, real LKR cost over time,
  efficiency/range trend, cost-per-km actuals (not a one-time estimate).
- No app ties EV running cost into the owner's **whole-vehicle** record (alongside service,
  documents, compliance renewals) — chargeNET is a charging utility, not an ownership app.
- No app connects the EV cohort to the **enthusiast garage** (direction 1) or **compliance
  guardian** (direction 2).

### Implication for DriveVault
- **Don't rebuild charging-station maps** — chargeNET/Place to Plug own that; if anything, link
  out or let owners note their go-to stations.
- **Do own the ownership side:** per-charge logs (kWh + cost), marginal running cost over time
  (the induwara calculator does this once — we do it continuously from real data), efficiency
  trend, and roll it into the same vehicle record as everything else.
- **Distribution:** EV Club SL + EVusersSriLanka Facebook groups are a ready-made, concentrated
  launch channel for direction 3.
