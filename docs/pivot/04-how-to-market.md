# DriveVault — Marketing Strategy

> Applies marketing ideas #s from the 139-idea library where referenced.

## Overarching principle

LinkedIn is unavailable as a channel. The SL car community is concentrated in a small number of high-trust rooms. The strategy is **seeding in the right rooms, not broadcasting broadly.** Quality of early users matters more than quantity — a car meet regular who shares their garage profile is worth more than 1,000 passive app store downloads.

---

## Pivot 1: Enthusiast Garage

### Traction assessment
**High word-of-mouth potential, low cold-start risk.** The shareable profile is the marketing artifact — every profile shared IS an ad. The challenge is seeding the first cohort of quality profiles that make others want one.

### Channels

**1. AutoLanka forums + Facebook group (#38 — Community seeding)**
- AutoLanka is the largest and most active SL car enthusiast hub; members already maintain project/build threads on the forum
- Seed the first 10–20 high-quality garage profiles with real cars from existing AutoLanka regulars (via DMs / permission)
- Post a "DriveVault garage profile" thread on AutoLanka — the format maps directly to existing build thread culture
- Comment in ongoing build threads linking to the builder's DriveVault profile (only if they've created one)
- *Why it fits:* AutoLanka users already create build logs in plain text — DriveVault formalises and makes it shareable

**2. Marque clubs: Honda Club SL, other active clubs (#64 — Partnership marketing)**
- Approach club admins: offer a "club garage" — a branded collection page showing all member cars
- Club-branded profile pages give admins something tangible to offer members
- Start with Honda Club SL (large, active, import-friendly post-ban)
- *Why it fits:* Clubs need member engagement tools; a digital garage fits naturally

**3. Car meets + events (#110 — Guerrilla / event marketing)**
- Print QR codes at a meet: "Scan to add your car to DriveVault"
- The shareable profile *is the flyer* — attendees can share their build profile as their car's "business card"
- Target Saturday morning meets at Galle Face, Viharamahadevi, club events
- *Why it fits:* Meet culture is inherently social and visual — a profile link replaces a phone wallpaper

**4. Instagram / TikTok / YouTube (#39 — Short-form video)**
- Garage profile reveals as short video content: "This is my car's DriveVault profile" — before/after of adding mods
- Build timelines are inherently visual — a timelapse of a build log is native short-form content
- Target SL automotive influencers / content creators (even micro, 5K–30K followers) for seeded posts
- *Why it fits:* CRLO produces nothing visual. DriveVault profiles are designed to be photographed and shared

**5. Waitlist + exclusivity launch (#81 — Early access / exclusivity)**
- Don't open to everyone at launch. Create a "founding garage" tier — first 500 cars added get a "Founding Member" badge on their public profile
- Invite-only first month: AutoLanka regulars and club members get invite codes
- *Why it fits:* Mimetic desire — people want what they can't have; exclusivity signals status in a status-conscious community

**6. Powered-by watermark on shared profiles (#87 — Powered-by marketing)**
- Public profile cards shared as images carry a subtle "Built on DriveVault" mark
- Every share on Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp is a passive impression
- *Why it fits:* Zero cost, scales with virality

### Traction where you'll win fastest
**AutoLanka forum threads.** The format maps exactly to existing build thread culture. Five great profiles posted by real AutoLanka regulars will generate more organic installs than any paid campaign.

## Pivot 2: Compliance Guardian

### Traction assessment
**High total addressable market, highly shareable single message.** Every car owner in Sri Lanka is a potential user. The message ("never pay a late penalty") is self-evident and requires no enthusiast context to land.

### Channels

**1. Facebook car owner groups (#38 — Community seeding)**
- Target mass-market owner groups: Colombo Car Owners, ikman/riyasewana audiences, buy/sell groups
- Single message: "This app reminds you 30 days before your revenue licence expires so you never pay the 10% late penalty." — no further explanation needed
- Share the reminder screenshot as social proof (blurred real data)
- *Why it fits:* Revenue licence anxiety is universal among SL car owners; the problem is instantly recognisable

**2. Engineering as marketing: free penalty calculator (#15 — Free tool)**
- Build a lightweight web page: "SL Revenue Licence Late Penalty Calculator" — enter annual tax, how many months late, get penalty amount in LKR
- SEO target: "revenue licence late fee Sri Lanka", "road tax penalty Sri Lanka"
- CTA from calculator: "Never calculate this again — DriveVault reminds you before it's due"
- *Why it fits:* High-intent search (people Google this when already late or worried); free tool builds trust and captures emails

**3. Easy keyword SEO (#1 — Low-competition keywords)**
- Target long-tail SL compliance searches: "when to renew revenue licence Sri Lanka", "emission test due date Sri Lanka", "fitness certificate renewal Sri Lanka"
- Blog posts answering exactly these questions; CTA is app download / reminder setup
- *Why it fits:* These are questions every SL car owner asks; currently answered by forums and outdated government pages — a clean, helpful answer ranks

**4. WhatsApp / Facebook organic sharing (#93 — Viral loops)**
- The reminder notification itself is shareable: "DriveVault just reminded me my revenue licence expires in 7 days — saved me from a 10% penalty"
- Design the reminder notification to be screenshot-shareable (clean layout, penalty amount visible)
- *Why it fits:* WhatsApp car owner groups are extremely active in SL — one shared screenshot in a group of 200 owners is high-reach

**5. Founder email + community posts (#47 — Founder-led outreach)**
- Personal posts in relevant FB groups from the founder: "I built this after paying a late renewal penalty — here's what it does"
- Authentic, low-cost, high-trust
- *Why it fits:* SL communities respond well to personal stories; a founder talking directly to owners cuts through noise

**6. Google App Store / Play Store ASO (#123 — App marketplace)**
- Target: "Sri Lanka vehicle reminder", "revenue licence reminder Sri Lanka", "car renewal app Sri Lanka"
- These are low-competition keywords with local intent — organic installs from users already looking
- *Why it fits:* Compliance is a search-driven category; people look for this when their renewal date approaches

### Traction where you'll win fastest
**Facebook car owner groups + the penalty calculator free tool.** Both reach mass-market owners (not enthusiasts) with a single, high-urgency message. The calculator captures email/intent before the app is even installed.
