# Pivot 10: Motorcycle & Three-Wheeler Mass Market

## One-line positioning

DriveVault becomes Sri Lanka's first mobile app built for the country's dominant vehicle classes — motorcycles and three-wheelers — with Sinhala-first compliance reminders (revenue licence, emission test, insurance), maintenance tracking, and a separate three-wheeler driver mode for income/cost management.

## Why this avoids competing with CRLO

CRLO is a generic, English-language, car-centric personal logbook with no SL localisation, no revenue-licence or emission-test compliance hooks, no Sinhala UI, and no three-wheeler driver economics mode. It is structurally designed for a market where the dominant vehicle is a car (US, Europe, Australia). Sri Lanka's dominant vehicles are motorcycles and three-wheelers — the polar opposite. Targeting these classes with deep localisation (SL compliance calendar, LKR costs, Sinhala labels, three-wheeler income tracking) creates a segment CRLO would have to rebuild from scratch to enter. Localisation and compliance depth in a high-volume low-ARPU market is not a gap a globally-focused indie app closes quickly.

## Market evidence (verified)

**Fleet dominance of motorcycles and three-wheelers**
- As of Q3 2023, Sri Lanka had approximately **4.85 million registered motorcycles** and **1.18 million registered three-wheelers** — together over 70% of the ~8.5M total vehicle fleet. Passenger cars number only ~0.90 million. Source: Department of Motor Traffic figures cited in [EconomyNext](https://economynext.com/sri-lanka-vehicle-registration-rise-to-35000-in-july-mobikes-almost-at-pre-crisis-levels-234977/) and [SriLankaMirror](https://srilankamirror.com/news/1826-new-vehicles-added-to-roads-daily-1213-motorcycles-and-386-cars/).
- In the pre-crisis period, Sri Lanka was adding **1,213 new motorcycles per day** net, versus 386 cars. Motorcycles are the mass-market vehicle, not the niche. Source: [SriLankaMirror](https://srilankamirror.com/news/1826-new-vehicles-added-to-roads-daily-1213-motorcycles-and-386-cars/).
- Post-import-ban-lift (February 2025), motorcycle registrations in July 2025 alone hit **26,171** — almost at pre-crisis peaks — indicating a large cohort of new motorcycle owners entering the market now. Source: [EconomyNext](https://economynext.com/sri-lanka-vehicle-registration-rise-to-35000-in-july-mobikes-almost-at-pre-crisis-levels-234977/).
- The import ban, lasting ~5 years, suppressed registrations artificially; the fleet is now growing rapidly again, with new vehicle registrations for all of 2025 (through November) hitting **312,317**, up from 74,410 in 2024. Source: [Sunday Times](https://www.sundaytimes.lk/260111/news/revenue-soars-after-sri-lanka-lifts-vehicle-import-ban-627703.html).

**Three-wheeler economic scale**
- Over **1.06 million three-wheelers registered** islandwide by end of 2016 (pre-crisis; current fleet likely similar after recovery). Source: [The Morning — Inside Sri Lanka's tuktuk economy](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/c3OqGTjRfyzr8lo4Ois8).
- The sector supports approximately **9.2% of Sri Lanka's national working population** — roughly **760,000 three-wheeler-connected households**, supporting 2.8 million people. Source: [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/c3OqGTjRfyzr8lo4Ois8).
- Three-wheelers contribute **35% of the transport sector's GDP contribution** (transport = 7% of national GDP). Source: [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/c3OqGTjRfyzr8lo4Ois8).
- **92% of three-wheeler drivers are self-employed, full-time owners** — these are micro-businesses, not hobbyists. Source: [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/c3OqGTjRfyzr8lo4Ois8).
- **85% purchased via leasing or bank loans**; **98% use formal banking** for savings. Drivers are financially sophisticated, not unbanked. Source: [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/c3OqGTjRfyzr8lo4Ois8).
- **66% of full-time three-wheeler drivers earn over Rs. 40,000/month**; mean income Rs. 46,000; high earners exceed Rs. 100,000. PickMe-registered drivers average ~Rs. 140,000/month at their peak. Source: [The Morning](https://www.themorning.lk/articles/c3OqGTjRfyzr8lo4Ois8); [Global Press Journal](https://globalpressjournal.com/asia/sri_lanka/sri-lankan-three-wheeler-drivers-get-boost-new-mobile-apps-demand-service/).
- **Over 400,000 drivers registered** with the All Ceylon Three Wheelers Association as of 2016. Source: [Global Press Journal](https://globalpressjournal.com/asia/sri_lanka/sri-lankan-three-wheeler-drivers-get-boost-new-mobile-apps-demand-service/).

**Compliance requirements creating annual forced touchpoints**
- Every vehicle in Sri Lanka requires an annual **revenue licence** renewal, which in turn requires a valid **emission test certificate** and a valid **insurance certificate** to be processed. Source: [Department of Motor Traffic — eRevenue Licence](https://web.erl2.gov.lk/); [gazette.lk](https://www.gazette.lk/2023/10/online-vehicle-revenue-license-renewal-e-revenue-licence-gov-lk.html).
- Online renewal is available at the official eRL portal but is under-utilised; most owners still queue in person. Source: [ICTA](http://ictawebstg.lgcc.gov.lk/projects/online-revenue-license-issuance/).
- Missing a revenue licence deadline incurs a **10% surcharge up to 3 months late, 20% beyond that**. Source: [gazette.lk](https://www.gazette.lk/2023/10/online-vehicle-revenue-license-renewal-e-revenue-licence-gov-lk.html).
- These deadlines create a **mandatory annual engagement touchpoint** that generic global apps (CRLO, Fuelly, MotorManage) never surface. DriveVault's existing document-vault + expiry banner infrastructure already handles this.

**App adoption readiness**
- **19M+ smartphones** in SL; **87% Android**; mobile:desktop ratio 4:1. Source: [DataReportal Digital 2024 Sri Lanka](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-sri-lanka); [Statista SL OS share](https://www.statista.com/statistics/931178/sri-lanka-mobile-os-share/).
- PickMe (ride-hailing app) had **1 million bookings within 7 months** of launch in 2015, demonstrating that three-wheeler drivers adopt useful apps quickly. Source: [Global Press Journal](https://globalpressjournal.com/asia/sri_lanka/sri-lankan-three-wheeler-drivers-get-boost-new-mobile-apps-demand-service/).
- Sri Lanka Motorcycle Riders Association has an active Facebook presence; multiple dedicated Facebook groups for motorcycle, EV, and vehicle communities exist. Source: [Facebook — Sri Lanka Motorcycle Riders Association](https://www.facebook.com/SriLankaMotorcycleRidersAssociation/).

## Target user & Sri Lanka relevance

**User A — Motorcycle owner (mass market)**
- Age 18–40, male, urban or peri-urban, Android user.
- Uses their motorcycle as primary transport; pays for petrol, occasional chain/oil service, and the annual revenue licence + emission test.
- Currently uses no app to track any of this. Tracks costs (if at all) in a notebook or WhatsApp message-to-self.
- Pain: misses revenue licence deadline, forgets when oil was last changed, no record if selling the bike.
- Population: **~4.85 million registered motorcycles**; even 5% digitally active = **242,000 potential users**.

**User B — Three-wheeler driver (micro-business mode)**
- Self-employed, full-time, earning Rs. 40,000–100,000/month. Has a leasing payment, daily fuel cost, weekly maintenance, and needs to know whether today was a profitable day.
- Currently tracks income in a paper logbook or not at all; leasing repayment is a fixed anxiety.
- Pain: no easy way to see if income is covering fuel + leasing + maintenance, no digital service record for the vehicle, misses renewal deadlines.
- Population: **~800,000 actively operating three-wheelers**; even 3% paying users = **24,000 potential subscribers**.

**Sri Lanka relevance**: These are the two largest vehicle classes in SL by a wide margin. Every globally-available maintenance app is built for cars in English. No app addresses the SL revenue-licence/emission-test compliance cycle in Sinhala or provides a three-wheeler income/cost P&L.

## User acquisition

**For motorcycle owners**
1. **Facebook / Sinhala content marketing** (zero cost): Post Sinhala-language short-form videos in the Sri Lanka Motorcycle Riders Association page and motorcycle groups. Format: "Is your revenue licence expiring soon? Here's how to track it on your phone." Each video teaches something useful and ends with a download link. Sinhala-language automotive content is scarce and highly shareable.
2. **Petrol station partnerships**: A4 flyers at Lanka IOC / Ceypetco stations ("Track your bike's fuel on your phone — free"). Low-cost physical distribution. Station managers are often open to this for free.
3. **Motorcycle importer tie-in**: New motorcycle importers and dealers (active again post-ban-lift 2025) can bundle a QR code / app card with every purchase. Pitch to dealers as a value-add ("helps your customers remember first service"). Free co-distribution.
4. **Google Play / App Store ASO in Sinhala**: Very low competition for Sinhala-language automotive keywords. Rank for terms like "රථ රක්ෂිත බලපත්‍ර මතක් කිරීම" (vehicle licence reminder).

**For three-wheeler drivers**
1. **All Ceylon Three Wheelers Association**: Direct outreach to their leadership. Offer a free bulk trial for 500 registered drivers. Associations are actively looking for tools to professionalise their members.
2. **PickMe driver network**: Three-wheeler drivers who already use PickMe are digital-native and income-motivated. Reach them via PickMe Driver community channels or Facebook groups.
3. **WhatsApp group seeding**: Three-wheeler driver WhatsApp groups are well-established (income-driven, trade association communities). A demo video of the income tracker shared by one trusted driver goes wide organically.

**First action**: Record a 60-second Sinhala-language screen-record demo of "adding your bike and setting a revenue licence reminder" and post it in the SL Motorcycle Riders Association Facebook group. Measure downloads in 72 hours before any other investment.

## Virality

**The built-in sharing loop:**

1. **Revenue licence reminder sharing**: A motorcycle owner gets a push notification 30 days before their revenue licence expires. They share a screenshot in their family or friend WhatsApp group: *"Oya magenta app eke bike details dammoth oka remind karanawa" ("put your bike in this app and it reminds you").* The concrete, practical utility drives word-of-mouth in a context where forgetting the renewal is a real, common, financially painful mistake.

2. **Resale record sharing**: When selling a used motorcycle on ikman.lk, owners can share a DriveVault vehicle summary (service history, fuel records, document history) as a public link. Buyers trust records over seller claims; a shareable history is a selling point worth Rs. 5,000–20,000 in negotiation. The buyer then downloads the app.

3. **Three-wheeler driver P&L sharing**: Drivers compare daily earnings in community groups. A screenshot of "today I cleared Rs. 3,200 after fuel and maintenance" from a DriveVault income card is social currency among drivers. This is how PickMe's driver earnings spread virally — drivers love quantifying their work.

**Why it's particularly strong here**: Motorcycle riders in SL are a community, not just a demographic. They ride together, share garages, discuss bikes on Facebook. A tool one rider finds useful spreads immediately through their network. The revenue licence reminder is not a nice-to-have — it's an anxiety that every rider shares.

## Revenue model & potential

### Model

**Free tier** (motorcycle): core vehicle tracking, one revenue-licence reminder, basic fuel log. Enough to demonstrate value before asking for payment.

**Pro — Motorcycle** (Rs. 290/month or Rs. 2,490/year):
- Unlimited vehicles
- All document expiry reminders (emission, insurance, driving licence)
- Maintenance history with reminder scheduling
- Fuel cost analytics (LKR/km, monthly spend chart)
- Shareable resale record link

**Pro — Three-Wheeler Driver** (Rs. 490/month or Rs. 3,990/year):
- All Motorcycle Pro features
- Daily income logging (trip count, gross fare, app-commission deduction)
- Daily expense logging (fuel, maintenance, leasing payment)
- Net daily/monthly P&L dashboard
- Leasing repayment tracker (balance, monthly payment, projected payoff)

**Affiliate / partnership streams (non-subscription)**
- **Insurance affiliate**: Push a renewal reminder → link to a partner insurer (Allianz, SLI, HNB Assurance). Commission: estimated Rs. 200–500/policy.
- **Emission test reminders → LAUGFS EcoSri / Clean Co affiliate**: Link to nearest VET centre; potential referral arrangement.
- **Spare parts / accessories**: Curated affiliate links to ikman or Amazon parts listings within maintenance logs.

### TAM math

- **Motorcycle TAM**: 4.85M motorcycles. Assume 10% smartphone-active, engaged users over 3 years = 485,000 addressable. At 5% paid conversion = **24,250 subscribers × Rs. 2,490/year = Rs. 60.4M ARR** (aspirational 3-year ceiling).
- **Three-wheeler TAM**: 800K active vehicles. 15% smartphone-engaged = 120,000 addressable (three-wheeler drivers are already digital via PickMe). At 5% paid = **6,000 subscribers × Rs. 3,990/year = Rs. 23.9M ARR** (aspirational 3-year ceiling).
- **Combined realistic Year 3 target** (5% of addressable): ~Rs. 84M ARR — but this requires significant network effects.

### Conservative Year 1 scenario

- 3,000 free installs from Sinhala Facebook content + ASO
- 5% paid conversion = **150 paying subscribers**
- Blended ARPU Rs. 3,000/year (mix of monthly + annual)
- **Year 1 ARR: Rs. 450,000 (~USD 1,500)**
- + Affiliate commissions (insurance, ~50 referrals × Rs. 300): Rs. 15,000
- **Total Year 1: ~Rs. 465,000**

### Conservative Year 2 scenario (word-of-mouth + association partnership)

- 20,000 installs; 8% paid conversion = **1,600 paying subscribers**
- Blended ARPU Rs. 3,200/year
- **Year 2 ARR: Rs. 5.12M (~USD 17.5K)**
- + Affiliates (~500 referrals × Rs. 350): Rs. 175,000
- **Total Year 2: ~Rs. 5.3M**

Break-even on Cloud Run + Neon + Firebase costs (~Rs. 50K/month) occurs around 200–250 paying subscribers.

## Feature roadmap by phase

### Phase 1 / MVP (existing DriveVault + motorcycle-specific adaptations)
- **Already built**: Vehicle garage (supports motorcycles/three-wheelers as vehicle types), fuel logs (LKR, litres), document vault with expiry reminders and CredentialExpiryBanner, driving credentials, maintenance logging, dashboard with upcoming-renewals panel.
- **Net-new for MVP (motorcycle focus)**:
  - Sinhala-language strings for all key screens (revenue licence reminder, fuel log, maintenance — at minimum the notification copy).
  - Motorcycle-specific vehicle type defaults (engine CC field instead of cubic capacity as optional; chain vs oil service reminder presets).
  - Revenue licence + emission + insurance expiry as prominently surfaced first-class items on dashboard (not buried in documents) — tailored copy for SL compliance calendar.
  - Shareable vehicle summary page (public URL showing service history, no sensitive data).

### Phase 2
- **Three-wheeler driver mode**: toggle in profile activates income/expense dashboard.
  - Daily income entry: trip count, gross fare, net after PickMe/InDrive/Uber commission deduction.
  - Daily expense entry: fuel (pre-fill from fuel log), maintenance, misc.
  - Net P&L card: today / this week / this month.
  - Leasing tracker: loan amount, monthly payment, remaining balance, payoff countdown.
- **Insurance affiliate integration**: Revenue licence reminder deep-links to partner insurer quote flow.
- **Emission test centre finder**: Map of VET centres (Clean Co, LAUGFS EcoSri) near current location, with "Add to calendar" for test booking.
- **Community-facing resale record**: Improved shareable link with QR code for ikman listings.

### Phase 3
- Push notification localisation: Sinhala + Tamil language support for all reminders.
- Three-wheeler association bulk onboarding: admin panel for association reps to enrol member vehicles in bulk.
- Fuel price integration: auto-pull current Lanka IOC / Ceypetco pump prices (public data) so fuel-cost calculation is pre-filled.
- Gamification for drivers: monthly "Clean Run" badge (full compliance, service on time), shareable on Facebook — drives organic visibility.
- Motorcycle community features: anonymous fuel-economy comparison by model (e.g., Honda CB150 fleet average in SL) — a lightweight data network effect.

## Risks & moat

**Risks**

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Low willingness-to-pay at Rs. 290/month in a price-sensitive market | High | Free tier must deliver real value (revenue licence reminder alone saves Rs. 500–3,000 in late fees); annual plan at Rs. 2,490 amortises to Rs. 208/month psychologically | 
| Sinhala localisation quality — machine translation looks bad | Medium | Use a native Sinhala speaker (freelancer, Rs. 5,000–15,000 one-time) for UI strings; community corrections via feedback |
| Low smartphone penetration among older/rural motorcycle owners | Medium | Target urban 18–35 cohort first; ASO + Facebook reach them; rural expansion later |
| CRLO or a well-funded player localises to SL quickly | Low | CRLO has no SL presence, no Sinhala, no compliance hooks; a global app localising to a small market is slow and commercially unattractive for them |
| Three-wheeler drivers distrust new apps after bad experiences | Medium | Trust through association endorsement; free trial with zero friction (no credit card, no account required for basic reminder) |

**Moat**
- **Compliance data depth**: Revenue licence expiry dates, emission cert, insurance records stored in DriveVault create switching cost; a rider who has 3 years of records here does not start over.
- **Language wedge**: A polished Sinhala-first vehicle app is a durable barrier for any English-language global competitor; replicating it requires genuine SL investment.
- **Three-wheeler driver economics**: The income/expense tracking layer for micro-businesses is a niche no global app addresses; once a driver builds 6 months of P&L history in DriveVault, churn is very low.
- **Fleet breadth**: Supporting motorcycles + three-wheelers + cars means a family with all three vehicle types consolidates on DriveVault; breadth increases household ARPU and reduces churn.

**Bottom line**: This is the highest-reach pivot available to DriveVault — 6 million vehicles, 760,000 three-wheeler livelihoods, and zero localised SL-compliant apps exist. The viral and acquisition paths are concrete and low-cost (Sinhala Facebook content, association partnerships). The primary risk is converting a price-sensitive market to paid; the mitigation is a genuinely useful free tier anchored to the revenue-licence reminder — a pain point with a real financial consequence that makes the value proposition undeniable.
