# Pivot 3: Enthusiast Garage + ShowBoard QR

## One-line positioning

A shareable digital spec card for Sri Lankan car enthusiasts — scan the windshield QR at any car meet and see the full build, spec, and story of the car in front of you.

---

## Why this avoids competing with CRLO

CRLO explicitly positions itself as "made for the pump, not the desk — deliberately unglamorous." Its sharing model is collaborative (invite family, mechanic, co-driver to a private garage) not performative. CRLO has no public profile, no discoverable build timeline, no enthusiast identity layer, and no physical artifact (sticker/tag) that bridges the offline car-meet world to the digital profile. It cannot pivot to this without abandoning its "unglamorous utility" brand. DriveVault's existing vehicle, photo, and document infrastructure is a 70% head start on what enthusiasts actually need.

The forum hosts dedicated brand sections (Honda, Toyota, Mitsubishi, BMW, Mercedes, Land Rover), project-car blogs, and a Car Shows & Events gallery. Honda Club Sri Lanka was directly formed out of an AutoLanka-organized meet.
- **Instagram**: [@srilankanscarzone](https://www.instagram.com/srilankanscarzone) has **18,000+ followers** on content centred on car spottings and community posts. [@srilanka_carmeetup_](https://www.instagram.com/srilanka_carmeetup_/) documents car meets. JDM and luxury car content creators (Lanka Street Kings, @nipulwithcars, @SriLankaCarSpotters) are active on TikTok.
- **Facebook**: Cars and Coffee Sri Lanka ([facebook.com/carsncoffeesl](https://www.facebook.com/carsncoffeesl/)) — dedicated event community. Old Car Lovers Club, Vehicle Club in Sri Lanka, and marque-specific groups are also active.

### Events (scale proof)

- **Colombo Motor Show 2025** runs three editions across the year (March, June, November) with an expected audience of **500,000** across all events. ([colombomotorshow.lk](https://colombomotorshow.lk/), [10times.com](https://10times.com/colombo-motor-show/visitors))
- **Motor Show 2025 at BMICH** (May 23–25): 30+ exhibitors, **50,000+ expected visitors**. ([motorshow.lk](https://motorshow.lk/))
- AutoLanka gallery documents Car Culture Motor Show 2025 (May 3rd), SR Motor Show, Street2Track events — regular cadence of enthusiast gatherings.

### The "boomer board" gap

Physical custom car show boards remain the current norm. Companies like Boards by Nick and Car Show Signs Illustrated sell printed spec boards for USD 30–120. These can't be updated, can't link to video, and spectators cannot interact with them. AutoBio Tags ([autobiotags.com](https://www.autobiotags.com)) is the closest precedent — a US startup that received a patent in 2021 for a QR+NFC windshield cling that links to a digital car profile. It has no meaningful scale outside the US collector car niche, charges $10 for replacement tags, and has published no user numbers. No equivalent exists for Asia or for the Sri Lankan market. The concept is proven to resonate with enthusiasts; nobody has executed it at scale in this region.

### Import surge creates new aspirational buyers

Vehicle registrations surged from 74,410 in 2024 to **312,317 by November 2025** after the import ban was lifted in February 2025, with USD 2.04 billion in vehicle import expenditure — the third-highest annual spend on record. ([adaderana.lk](https://adaderana.lk/news.php?nid=121742), [economynext.com](https://economynext.com/sri-lanka-imports-us107mn-of-vehicles-in-april-2025-223583/)). Thousands of first-time or returning car owners with newly-imported vehicles are a natural enthusiast recruitment pool.

## Target user & Sri Lanka relevance

**Primary user: the Sri Lankan "project car" owner / serious enthusiast** — someone who has modified, imported, or lovingly maintained a car they are proud of and want others to recognise. They post on AutoLanka, attend Cars and Coffee, follow JDM content on TikTok, and currently show their car with a printed A4 spec sheet or nothing at all.

**Secondary user: the meet spectator / aspiring enthusiast** — scans a QR on a windshield, sees the build, follows the car, and is introduced to DriveVault.

**Tertiary: marque club coordinators** — Honda Club SL, Mercedes Club SL, etc. — who need a shared space to list all member cars.

This is a segment CRLO structurally cannot serve: it is identity-driven, visual, social, and requires a discoverable public artifact. The entire loop — scan QR → see profile → download app — is foreign to CRLO's design philosophy.

## User acquisition

### First 30 days (pre-launch seeding)

1. **AutoLanka forum post** — "I built an app so you can put your car's full build on a QR you stick to your windshield — feedback wanted." No LinkedIn required; this is the right audience. Target the Project Cars sub-forum and Car Shows & Events section.
2. **Cars and Coffee Sri Lanka Facebook** — post a demo video of scanning a windshield QR at a meet.
3. **DM 5–10 prominent SL automotive Instagram/TikTok creators** (18K–100K reach) with a free Premium code. One authentic post from @srilankanscarzone reaches more qualified leads than any paid campaign.

### First 90 days (seeding at physical meets)

4. **Attend or sponsor a car meet** — arrive with a car (your own or a friend's) already running the ShowBoard QR sticker. Spectators who scan a real QR in the wild and get a beautiful profile are the best demo. Each car with a sticker is a walking advertisement.
5. **Partner with one marque club** (Honda Club SL is the easiest given AutoLanka's history) — offer a free club page where all member vehicles appear. Clubs benefit; you get seeding with their most active members.
6. **SEO / content** — "How to make a digital spec card for your car in Sri Lanka" targets AutoLanka readers and general search. No LinkedIn needed.

### Ongoing

- Word-of-mouth from the QR sticker artifact (see Virality below) is the primary organic engine.
- AutoLanka forum signature links drive passive discovery.
- Marque club partnerships bring new cohorts of enthusiasts with each new club onboarded.

## Virality

**The artifact is the loop.** A printed QR windshield cling attached to a car at a meet automatically generates curiosity from every passerby. The loop:

1. Enthusiast puts a DriveVault ShowBoard QR sticker on their windshield before a meet.
2. A spectator at the meet scans it with their phone camera — no app required to view.
3. They see the car's full build: photos, spec sheet, mod timeline, owner's note.
4. At the bottom of the profile: "Create your own — DriveVault."
5. Spectator downloads, creates a profile, orders a sticker.
6. Their car becomes another node in the loop.

**Secondary loop**: Profile links are shared on WhatsApp and Instagram ("check out my car's full build"). Each share is a branded link that lands on a beautiful public profile. The profile IS the marketing artifact.

**Network effect (weak but real)**: Club pages aggregate member cars. A club sharing their page for a meet creates multi-car exposure in one post.

This is a fundamentally different virality mechanism from CRLO (which has no public artifact). It requires a physical presence in the world (the sticker), making it impossible for a digital-only competitor to replicate without the same grassroots meet presence.

## Revenue model & potential

### Model: Freemium + Physical Merchandise

| Tier | What you get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 1 vehicle, 10 photos, basic spec card, public ShowBoard profile | LKR 0 |
| Enthusiast Premium | 5 vehicles, unlimited photos, mod timeline, custom profile theme, analytics (who scanned) | LKR 590/month or LKR 4,900/year |
| ShowBoard QR Sticker Kit | Printed windshield cling (weather-resistant vinyl) + NFC option | LKR 1,500 (sticker) / LKR 2,500 (sticker + NFC) |
| Club Page | Branded club/group page listing all member vehicles + event section | LKR 7,500/year per club |
| Event Featured Spot | Prominent placement in a featured grid at a designated car show partner event | LKR 5,000/event |

### Rough TAM math (Sri Lanka only, conservative)

- AutoLanka + Cars & Coffee + Instagram automotive community estimated addressable pool: **50,000–80,000 active enthusiasts** (AutoLanka has 120K posts in one section alone; Instagram @srilankanscarzone has 18K, broader reach is much larger).
- Realistic app downloads in year 1 (seeded via meets + forum): **3,000–5,000 users**.
- Conversion to Enthusiast Premium at 8%: **240–400 paid users**.
- At LKR 4,900/year average: **LKR 1.18M–1.96M recurring/year**.
- Sticker orders (40% of active users order one, avg LKR 1,800): 1,200–2,000 orders = **LKR 2.16M–3.6M one-time/year**.
- Club pages (10 clubs at LKR 7,500): **LKR 75,000/year**.

**Conservative year-1 revenue: LKR 3.4M–5.6M (~USD 11,000–18,500)**

This is not large by any absolute measure, but it is achievable by a solo developer with zero paid acquisition cost. By year 3, if 15,000 active users are in the app (realistic given import surge and growing enthusiast scene), revenue could reach LKR 12M–20M/year.

### Why sticker revenue matters

Physical merchandise has near-100% gross margin after unit costs (~LKR 400–600 to print and mail). It also increases stickiness — a user who has printed their sticker is unlikely to delete the app. The sticker is also a customer acquisition tool (see Virality), making it double-purpose.

## Risks & moat

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Enthusiasts are vocal but don't pay | High | Physical sticker purchase de-risks this — you know they'll pay LKR 1,500 for a sticker at a meet. Premium features then feel cheap by comparison. |
| Instagram already serves the "show off your car" need | Medium | Instagram is a follower-based content feed. The ShowBoard profile is a scannable, spec-first, always-updated card anchored to the physical car. These are complementary, not competing — enthusiasts already cross-post everywhere. |
| CRLO adds a public profile | Low | Their entire brand is "unglamorous utility." Adding a public photo-first enthusiast mode contradicts their positioning and design system. Even if they did, they have no SL community presence or sticker distribution. |
| Chicken-and-egg: empty community on launch | Medium | Solved by seeding with real stickers at physical meets before a public launch. Profile quality at day 1 matters more than count. |
| Thin IP / moat | Medium | The sticker distribution network + club partnerships + community reputation (AutoLanka) are defensible in SL at small scale. The app is not globally defensible — but global is not the goal yet. |
| Solo developer build time | Low | Phase 1 MVP is 70% built. The public profile page + QR generation is a well-understood web/backend addition. Estimated 2–3 weeks of focused work. |

## Viability verdict

| Dimension | Score /10 | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | **7** | Real channels exist (AutoLanka, car meets, content creators); no paid spend required; sticker is a physical acquisition engine |
| Virality | **8** | Windshield QR loop is the best built-in viral mechanic of all pivots considered — works offline, creates real-world curiosity |
| Monetization | **5** | Enthusiasts are a willing but small audience; revenue in year 1 is LKR 3–6M (modest); sticker + club revenue diversifies beyond subs |
| Defensibility | **6** | Community moat and sticker distribution are real but thin; global replication is easy; SL-specific moat is the realistic goal |
| Build effort | **8** | 70% of data layer already exists; MVP is a public profile page + QR generator on top of existing vehicle/photo infrastructure |

**Bottom line:** This is the most achievable pivot for a solo developer because the data layer is already built and the acquisition channel (car meets + AutoLanka) is free, targeted, and accessible. Revenue will be modest in year 1 but the viral loop is the strongest of any pivot — every ShowBoard sticker in a windshield is a DriveVault advertisement that works without the developer doing anything.
