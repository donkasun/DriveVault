## How to read this (the scores don't tell the whole story)

Raw totals favour cheap-to-build consumer products. But strategy is about *fit*, not just totals. Three lenses matter more:

### By "highest monetization potential"
1. **Used-Car Buyer's Companion (8)** — per-check transactional fee on a high-stakes purchase; people pay readily.
2. **Fuel Optimizer / Fleet / Compliance / Doc Wallet / Service (7)** — recurring subscription with clear ROI.
- Lowest: Provenance, Enthusiast, Club, Motorcycle (5) — value is real but infrequent or price-sensitive.

### By "best built-in virality"
1. **Provenance buyer link (9)** — every used-car listing carrying a DriveVault link is a passive impression.
2. **ShowBoard QR / Fleet P&L card / Club event cards / Motorcycle (8)** — concrete, shareable artifacts.

### By "strongest moat / defensibility"
1. **Provenance (9)** — compounding history depth + trust.
2. **Compliance / Fuel / Club (8)** — SL-rules schema, data gravity, or network effects global apps can't copy.

### By "cheapest to ship now" (most reuses existing DriveVault)
1. **Compliance / Fuel / Enthusiast / Doc Wallet (8)** — 70%+ already built.
- Hardest: **Club (4)** and **Service Marketplace (5)** — substantial net-new, two-sided builds.

## Strategic read — these aren't 10 separate products

The strongest pivots cluster into **two complementary engines**, exactly the pattern that beats CRLO:

- **Daily-utility / monetization engine:** Compliance Guardian (1) + Fuel Optimizer (6) + Doc Wallet (7). All reuse existing infrastructure, all give a recurring reason to open the app, all are SL-defensible. This is your retention + subscription base.
- **Virality / acquisition engine:** Provenance link (2), ShowBoard QR (3), and the Used-Car Buyer's funnel (4). These create shareable artifacts that pull new users in for free.

Pivots 8 (Club) and 9 (Marketplace) are the highest-ceiling but highest-cost — **Phase 2+ plays** that need either revenue or a co-founder first. Pivots 5 (Fleet) and 10 (Motorcycle) are distinct *audience* bets that can be layered later by re-targeting the same engine.

## Recommended sequence (solo dev, no marketing budget)

1. **Ship Compliance Guardian first** (Pivot 1). Cheapest to build, daily relevance, quantifiable LKR ROI, proves willingness-to-pay — your biggest open question — fastest.
2. **Layer the Fuel/Running-Cost Optimizer** (Pivot 6) and its **free SEO web calculator** as the compounding acquisition channel.
3. **Add the Provenance buyer link** (Pivot 2) as the viral loop once you have a retained base — every shared link markets the app for free.
4. **Used-Car Buyer's Companion** (Pivot 4) as the highest-revenue standalone funnel if you want a transactional cash engine alongside subscriptions.
5. Defer Club (8) and Marketplace (9) until funded.

**Bottom line:** Lead with Compliance + Fuel (utility/retention), bolt on Provenance + ShowBoard QR (virality), keep Marketplace/Club as funded Phase-2 ceiling plays. None of these fight CRLO head-on.
