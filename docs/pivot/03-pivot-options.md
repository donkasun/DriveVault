# DriveVault — Pivot Options

> Two directions chosen. Decision recorded 2026-06-25.

## The two chosen pivots

### Pivot 1: Enthusiast Garage
**"Your car's digital identity — not just a logbook."**

A per-car profile that car enthusiasts are proud to show, not just a cost tracker.

**Core features (MVP):**
- Shareable public car profile ("digital spec card"): make, model, year, mods, photos, stats
- Build / modification log per car (dated entries, photo attachments, cost tracking)
- Project / build timeline — visual history of work done on the car
- Profile URL / QR code shareable on forums, at meets, on social

**Why this wins:**
- CRLO explicitly refuses this segment ("unglamorous")
- Enthusiasts are vocal, community-connected, and generate organic word-of-mouth
- A shareable profile *is* the marketing artifact — every shared profile is an ad
- AutoLanka, Honda Club SL, car meets are concentrated, reachable channels
- Builds directly on existing vehicle/fuel/expense data — no schema rebuild required

**Differentiation strength: High**
CRLO cannot copy this without abandoning its core positioning. A global logbook adding "glamour" is a brand contradiction.

**Traction potential: Medium-high**
Smaller total addressable audience (enthusiasts ≠ all owners) but disproportionate word-of-mouth amplification. First 100 enthusiast users are worth 1,000 passive users in terms of growth.

---

### Pivot 2: Sri Lanka Compliance Guardian
**"Never miss a renewal. Never pay a late penalty."**

The only app that aggregates and proactively reminds for all SL vehicle compliance dates.

**Core features (MVP):**
- Unified expiry dashboard: revenue licence, insurance, emission test, fitness cert, driving licence/permits — all in one place
- Push notifications at 90 / 30 / 7 / 1 day before each expiry
- Penalty context in the reminder: "Revenue licence expires in 7 days — late penalty is 10% of annual tax (~Rs. X)"
- SL-specific compliance calendar awareness (eRevenue portal links, what to bring, where to go)

**Why this wins:**
- Sri Lanka's eRevenue portal only renews — it does not remind, does not aggregate dates
- No existing app covers all 4–5 compliance documents in one place
- Late penalties are 10% / 20% / 30% of annual tax — real, quantifiable money saved
- Universal audience: every car owner in Sri Lanka, not just enthusiasts
- Builds directly on DriveVault's existing driving credentials + document expiry work

**Differentiation strength: Very high**
CRLO is global. It structurally cannot model SL-specific renewal rules, penalty tiers, or eRevenue portal workflows. This is a permanent moat.

**Traction potential: High**
Every car owner in Sri Lanka is a potential user. "Never pay a late penalty again" is a shareable message in every car owner group on Facebook — no automotive enthusiasm required.

## Positioning relationship

| | Enthusiast Garage | Compliance Guardian |
|--|--|--|
| **Primary audience** | Car enthusiasts, modifiers, build community | Every car owner in Sri Lanka |
| **Role in strategy** | Brand, acquisition, viral loops | Retention, daily utility, monetization |
| **CRLO comparison** | They refuse this segment | They structurally can't do this |
| **Viral mechanism** | Shareable profiles, car meet QR codes | "Never pay a late penalty" shareability |
| **Build dependency** | New work (profiles, mod log) | Existing work extended (expiry + push) |

**Stack:** Lead with Pivot 1 for brand and community seeding. Monetize and retain with Pivot 2. Both together = a product CRLO cannot assemble.
